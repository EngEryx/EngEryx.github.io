About Me

Turning complex challenges into elegant solutions

I'm Eric Kirima, a technology entrepreneur based in Nairobi, Kenya. As the founder of Eryx Labs, I lead a team that builds enterprise-grade software solutions for African businesses.

Our work spans ISP billing and network platforms, mobile money, document AI, AI agents and business systems, for clients like Truth Wireless, Kenya Flower Council, TLP Clinic, Staffing Africa and Manpower Networks.

I believe in building technology that's not just functional, but transformative—systems that scale with businesses and adapt to Africa's unique market demands.
